# Festival + Linz Open Data Project Brainstorm

Ideas for example projects in which the Ars Electronica Festival data drives
the experience and Linz open data materially changes the result.

| Project | Core experience | Festival data | Linz open data | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Festival Day Weaver** | Select events and generate a feasible itinerary with travel time, departure suggestions, water stops, and toilets. | Calendar, locations, recommended arrival | Transit lines, EFA journey planner, fountains, toilets | Medium |
| **The Shadiest Festival Route** | Find a walking route between venues that maximizes trees and passes drinking-water points. | Event times and venues | Tree registry, fountains | Medium |
| **Festival Weather Mode** | Adapt the program to heat, rain, or air quality by suggesting indoor events and more comfortable transfers. | Project categories, schedule, venues | Live weather and air-quality measurements | Medium |
| **Venue Time Machine** | Place festival venues over historical city maps and reveal how their surroundings changed. | Locations and project descriptions | Historical maps, orthophotos, current city map | Medium |
| **Streets Behind the Festival** | While walking to an event, tell the histories of the streets being crossed and connect them to the event's theme. | Project text, category, venue | Current and historical street names | Medium |
| **City/Festival Constellations** | Build an interactive network connecting countries, artists, projects, venues, and visitor-origin statistics. | Projects, contacts, countries, locations | Guest-origin tourism statistics | Medium |
| **Five-Minute Festival City** | Around each venue, visualize everything reachable during a 5-, 10-, or 15-minute break. | Venues and upcoming slots | Fountains, toilets, trees, Wi-Fi, playgrounds | Small |
| **Festival Mobility Pulse** | Animate event starts alongside historical cycling volumes and public-transport infrastructure. | Calendar and venue locations | Cycling counters, transit lines and stops | Medium |
| **Program in 3D** | Display events as glowing or growing forms over a 3D model of Linz; height can represent capacity or duration. | Venues, schedule, category, capacity | 3D city model | Large |
| **Festival vs. Everyday City** | Compare the geographic and temporal distribution of festival events with the wider Linz cultural program. | Calendar, categories, venues | Linztermine or Boudicca.Events | Medium |
| **Serendipity Route** | Create a route between selected events with one unexpected city-data detour chosen by theme. | Projects, contacts, schedule | Trees, edible hedges, historical streets, playgrounds | Medium |
| **Festival Infrastructure Stress Test** | Explore when many events finish simultaneously and which nearby transit stops, toilets, or walking corridors may see demand. | Calendar and locations | Transit network, toilets | Large |
